Our Story

A Hyderabad clothing studio, chosen piece by piece.

Sahasram opened its doors in Hyderabad in October 2025. Every kurta, set and kids’ outfit is picked by hand — for the print, the fall of the fabric and how it will feel to wear through a long, warm day. Most pieces come in a single size and colour, so what you take home is truly yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still unsure? We’re one WhatsApp message away.

Where is Sahasram boutique in Hyderabad?

On the 1st floor of Krishna Teja Classic, Plot 1247, Road No. 45, KPHB Colony, Hyderabad 500085 — opposite Prajay Megapolis. Call or WhatsApp 73372 21100.

Can I see a kurta set on a video call before buying?

Yes. Choose up to ten pieces on the website, pick a time, and Sharanya will show them to you on a WhatsApp video call before you decide.

How much do kurta sets cost at Sahasram?

Kurtas start under ₹1,000. Kurta sets range from about ₹1,600 for everyday cotton to ₹10,700 for silk occasion sets.

Do you ship outside Hyderabad?

Shipping across India is being set up for launch. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll help with your order in the meantime.

Do you have kids’ ethnic wear?

Yes — lehenga-cholis, Nehru jackets, dresses and festive sets for ages 0–8 and up.
